This Privacy Policy explains how Facecheck LLC ("Facecheck," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please uninstall and discontinue use.

1 What We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the App, provide features (e.g., maps, location-based functionality, local notifications, biometric login), and secure your account.

a) Information you provide

  • Account & Login: email address and password you submit to authenticate.
  • Support: content of messages you send to us, including contact details.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Device & Usage: device model, OS version, app version, language, time zone, basic diagnostic logs and crash events.
  • Network: IP address and approximate location derived from IP (for security and fraud prevention).
  • In‑App Preferences: settings such as theme, permissions toggles, and notification preferences.

c) Device features & permissions

  • Location (GPS, Wi‑Fi, cell): if you grant permission, the App may access precise or approximate location to power location features. You can disable this in OS settings.
  • Camera/Photos: if you grant permission, used to capture or select images. We do not create or store biometric templates from images.
  • Biometrics (Face/Touch ID): used only for local device authentication. We do not receive or store your biometric data.
  • Notifications: with your consent, we may send local notifications. You may disable notifications in OS settings.

d) Photo Attendance Records

  • Visual Attendance Photos: When you use the punch in/out feature, the App captures a photo as a visual timestamp record. These photos are:
  • • Used solely as attendance verification for your employer
  • • NOT analyzed for biometric identification or facial recognition
  • • Stored securely in encrypted AWS S3 storage
  • • Automatically deleted after 30 days
  • • Accessible only to authorized employer representatives

Important: We do NOT perform facial recognition, biometric analysis, or identity matching on photos. Photos serve only as visual attendance records, similar to a timestamped selfie.

NEW YORK CITY RESIDENTS - BIOMETRIC NOTICE

For New York City residents: We comply with NYC Administrative Code § 22-1201 (Local Law 3 of 2021) regarding biometric identifier information.

  • No Biometric Collection: We do NOT collect, retain, convert, store, or share biometric identifier information as defined by NYC law.
  • Photos Are NOT Biometric Data: The photos captured during punch in/out are simple image files used only as visual attendance timestamps. They are not analyzed, scanned, or converted into facial geometry, templates, or any biometric identifiers.
  • Your Rights: If our practices change to include biometric data collection, NYC residents will receive clear notice and the ability to opt-out as required by law.
  • Data Security: All photos are encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in AWS S3 with enterprise-grade security, and automatically deleted after 30 days.

Questions? NYC residents can contact us at 347-828-5790 for information about our data practices.

e) GPS Location Tracking Details

How Location Tracking Works:

  • When Collected: Location is captured ONLY during punch in/out events, not continuously tracked throughout the day.
  • What We Collect: Latitude, longitude, timestamp, and address (if available from GPS).
  • Why We Collect: To verify employees are at the designated work site when clocking in/out, as required by your employer.
  • Who Can See: Location data is shared with your employer's authorized administrators only.
  • Retention: Location records are retained as long as your employment record is active, or as required by labor law (typically 3-7 years depending on jurisdiction).
  • Your Control: You can disable location services in your device settings, but this may prevent you from using the punch in/out feature.

Note: Your employer requires location verification as a condition of using this time tracking system. Questions about this requirement should be directed to your employer.

Children: The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

13 Employee Rights & Workplace Monitoring (NY/NJ)

New York & New Jersey Employee Notice

If you are an employee in New York or New Jersey using this App as required by your employer, you have specific rights:

  • Right to Notice: Your employer must inform you about electronic monitoring, including GPS tracking and photo attendance features.
  • Employer Responsibility: Your employer, not Facecheck LLC, is responsible for complying with workplace monitoring laws and obtaining any required consents.
  • Data Access: You may request access to your own attendance records, location data, and photos by contacting your employer or calling us at 347-828-5790.
  • No Retaliation: Exercising your privacy rights should not result in retaliation. Report concerns to your employer's HR department or relevant labor authorities.
  • Union Members: If you are covered by a collective bargaining agreement, electronic monitoring may be subject to additional requirements. Consult your union representative.

Important: Facecheck LLC provides the technology platform. Your employer determines what data to collect and how to use it. We process data on behalf of your employer but do not make employment decisions or control workplace policies.
