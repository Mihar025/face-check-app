This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how Facecheck LLC handles subscription cancellations, refund requests, and billing disputes. We strive to be fair and transparent in all billing matters. Please read this policy carefully before purchasing any subscription or service.

1 Subscription Cancellation

How to Cancel Your Subscription

  • In-App Cancellation: Go to Settings → Subscription → Cancel Subscription
  • Web Portal: Log into your account dashboard and select "Manage Subscription"
  • Phone Support: Call +1 (347) 828-5790 during business hours

When Cancellation Takes Effect

  • Cancellation is effective at the end of your current billing period
  • You retain access to premium features until the end of the paid period
  • No charges will occur after the current billing period ends
  • Employee seats are automatically adjusted at the next billing cycle

Note: Cancelling your subscription does not delete your account or data. You can reactivate your subscription at any time.

2 Refund Eligibility

Eligible for Refund

  • First-time subscribers: Full refund within 7 days of initial purchase if no substantial use
  • Technical failures: Service unavailable for more than 72 consecutive hours
  • Billing errors: Duplicate charges, incorrect amounts, or unauthorized transactions
  • Feature misrepresentation: Advertised features not available as described
  • Account suspension errors: Wrongful suspension without Terms violation

Not Eligible for Refund

  • Partial period refunds: No refunds for unused time in billing period
  • Change of mind: After 7-day initial period for first-time users
  • Terms violations: Account suspended for violating Terms of Service
  • Price changes: Subscription price increases with proper notice
  • Third-party fees: Payment processing or currency conversion fees

3 Processing Times & Methods

Refund Processing Timeline

  • Request Review: 1-2 business days
  • Approval Notification: Within 3 business days
  • Payment Processing: 5-10 business days after approval
  • Bank Processing: Additional 3-5 business days may apply

Refund Methods

  • Original Payment Method: Default refund to the card/account used for purchase
  • Account Credit: Optional credit for future subscription periods
  • Alternative Methods: Bank transfer for special circumstances (fees may apply)

Important Processing Notes

  • Refunds cannot be processed to different payment methods without verification
  • International refunds may take up to 15 business days
  • Currency conversion rates apply at time of refund, not original purchase

4 App Store & Google Play Purchases

Purchases made through Apple App Store or Google Play Store are subject to their respective refund policies:

Apple App Store

  • Request refunds directly through Apple's Report a Problem page
  • Visit: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Apple determines refund eligibility based on their policies
  • Typical review time: 48 hours

Google Play Store

  • Request refunds through Google Play Order History
  • 48-hour window for immediate refunds
  • After 48 hours, contact Facecheck support for assistance
  • Google reviews requests based on their refund policy

Mobile Users: We cannot directly refund App Store or Google Play purchases. You must request through their platforms, but we can provide supporting documentation if needed.

5 Direct Stripe Payments

Web & Direct Subscriptions

For subscriptions purchased directly through our website using Stripe:

  • Full Control: We can process refunds directly
  • Faster Processing: Typically 3-5 business days
  • Partial Refunds: Available for technical issues

Payment Disputes & Chargebacks

  • Contact us before initiating a chargeback - we'll resolve it faster

  • Fraudulent chargebacks will result in permanent account termination
  • We reserve the right to challenge disputed transactions with evidence

6 Technical Issues & Service Credits

Qualifying Technical Issues

  • Complete Service Outage: App unavailable for all users
  • Data Loss: Permanent loss of user data due to our error
  • Critical Feature Failure: Core features (punch in/out, calculations) non-functional
  • Security Breach: Compromised account due to our security failure

Service Level Credits

4-24 hours 5% of monthly fee Automatic credit

Maintenance: Scheduled maintenance windows do not qualify for service credits. We provide 48-hour advance notice for planned downtime.

7 Dispute Resolution Process

How to File a Dispute

  1. Provide Information: Include receipt, date, amount, and reason for dispute
  2. Investigation: We review within 3 business days
  3. Resolution: Final decision communicated within 7 business days

Escalation Process

  • If unsatisfied with initial resolution, request manager review
  • Second-level review completed within 5 business days
  • Arbitration available per Terms of Service if needed

Fair Use Policy

Excessive refund requests or pattern of disputes may result in account review and potential service termination. We track refund history to prevent abuse.
