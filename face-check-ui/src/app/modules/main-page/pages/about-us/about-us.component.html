Our Story

Facecheck was founded in 2022 by a team of technology professionals who saw firsthand how outdated time tracking systems were holding businesses back.

After experiencing the frustration of manual timesheets, error-prone calculations, and lack of insights, our founders set out to build a solution that would revolutionize workforce management.

Today, Facecheck serves businesses across multiple industries, helping them save time, reduce errors, and gain valuable insights into their workforce productivity.
